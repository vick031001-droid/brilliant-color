How it works

The manager orders 100, 200, or 300 cases before learning the week’s demand. Sales are limited by both the order and customer demand.

Profit in one demand scenario

Cases sold × selling price
+ unsold cases × return credit
− cases ordered × purchase cost

Expected weekly profit

Multiply each scenario’s profit by its probability and add the three amounts. The order with the largest total is recommended. Tied orders are shown together.
